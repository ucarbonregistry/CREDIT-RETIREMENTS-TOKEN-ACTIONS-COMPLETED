# CREDIT-RETIREMENTS-TOKEN-ACTIONS-COMPLETED

A carbon credit retirement on UCR is the permanent removal of a carbon credit from circulation so it can no longer be sold or traded. Once a credit is retired, its associated climate benefit (reducing or removing 1 metric ton of CO_{2} is officially claimed by the retiree to offset their carbon footprint or any specific environmental claim. Since UCR assigns a unique serial number to every credit issued, the "retire" completes the life cycle of the credit.
