# CREDIT-RETIREMENTS-TOKEN-ACTIONS-COMPLETED

A carbon credit retirement on UCR is the permanent removal of a carbon credit from circulation so it can no longer be sold or traded. Once a credit is retired, its associated climate benefit (reducing or removing 1 metric ton of CO_{2} is officially claimed by the retiree to offset their carbon footprint or any specific environmental claim. Since UCR assigns a unique serial number to every credit issued, the "retire" completes the life cycle of the credit. This is an irreversible action. 

The "Burn and Tokenize" action (IS NOT A RETIREMENT, BUT A CANCEL AND TRANSFER EVENT) on the Universal Carbon Registry (UCR) is a native feature that allows an account holder to permanently remove an unretired carbon credit from the traditional UCR ledger and transfer it onto a blockchain network as a digital token. Unlike a standard retirement—which ends the credit's lifecycle to claim an environmental benefit—this action replaces the off-chain registry credit with an on-chain crypto asset. The metadata and the credit are permanently deleted ("burned") from the UCR database. The original credit is entirely removed from UCR's or holders control. This gives a 3rd party token developer or platform the green light to mint an identical digital token on a public blockchain network. The carbon credit now exists purely as a Real-World Asset (RWA) token on-chain. It can be openly traded, sold, or ultimately retired directly within the decentralized ecosystem. This is an irreversible action. 

The "Burn and Tokenize" action on the Universal Carbon Registry (UCR) transfers full ownership of the underlying unretired carbon credit asset to the token creator, moving it from the legacy database to the blockchain. This process grants the token creator total control, including the authority to mint a 1:1 token (or suitable fractions) and execute final retirement on-chain.
